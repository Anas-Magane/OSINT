# AlienVault OTX (Open Threat Exchange)

**Category:** Threat Intelligence

**Purpose:** Community-driven threat intelligence platform for sharing and searching indicators of compromise (IPs, domains, hashes, URLs) organized into "pulses" contributed by researchers and vendors.

**Website:** [otx.alienvault.com](https://otx.alienvault.com)

**Status:** ✅ Active (AT&T Cybersecurity / LevelBlue) | **License:** Free (account required); API available

**Installation:**

No installation required for manual lookups. An API and SDK are available for automation after creating a free account.

**Basic Usage:**

Search an indicator (IP, domain, hash, URL) at [otx.alienvault.com](https://otx.alienvault.com) to see related pulses and context.

**What it is useful for:**

- Contextualizing an indicator with community-contributed threat intelligence (campaign attribution, related indicators, malware families).
- Subscribing to curated pulses relevant to your sector for proactive monitoring.
- Cross-referencing findings from VirusTotal/AbuseIPDB with broader campaign context.

**Limitations:**

- Quality varies by contributor — pulses are not all equally vetted or current.
- Requires a free account for full API access.

**Operational Safety:**

Lookups are passive. When contributing your own pulses, avoid including sensitive internal network details alongside shared indicators.
