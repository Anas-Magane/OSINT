# Ahmia

**Category:** Dark Web / Onion Research

**Purpose:** A legitimate, filtered search engine for indexing and searching Tor hidden services (`.onion` sites), explicitly designed to exclude illegal content such as child abuse material.

**Website:** [ahmia.fi](https://ahmia.fi)

**Official Repository:** [github.com/ahmia/ahmia-site](https://github.com/ahmia/ahmia-site)

**Status:** ✅ Active | **License:** BSD-3-Clause

**Installation:**

No installation required — accessible via a regular browser at [ahmia.fi](https://ahmia.fi), or via [Tor Browser](tor-browser.md) for `.onion` result access.

**Basic Usage:**

Enter search terms at [ahmia.fi](https://ahmia.fi) to find indexed, policy-compliant onion services.

**What it is useful for:**

- A legal, research-oriented entry point into onion-network content, rather than manually guessing `.onion` addresses.
- Threat intelligence research into legitimate onion-hosted resources (e.g., privacy tools, news mirrors, research services).

**Limitations:**

- Coverage is limited to what Ahmia has crawled and chosen to index under its content policy.
- Does not index or facilitate access to illegal marketplaces or content by design — this repository does not provide alternatives that do.

**Operational Safety:**

Follow the same precautions as any onion research: use [Tor Browser](tor-browser.md) for actual `.onion` access, don't assume anonymity, and only access clearly legal content. See [Legal & Ethical Use](../../docs/legal-and-ethical-use.md#a-note-on-dark-web--onion-research).
