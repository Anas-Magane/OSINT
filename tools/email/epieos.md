# Epieos

**Category:** Email OSINT

**Purpose:** Web-based email lookup tool that reveals information linked to an email address (e.g., associated Google account name/photo, and registration traces on other services).

**Website:** [epieos.com](https://epieos.com)

**Status:** ✅ Active (hosted web service) | **License:** Proprietary (free tier + paid API)

**Installation:**

No installation required — it's a web application. An API is available for programmatic/authorized use; see the site for current terms.

**Basic Usage:**

Visit [epieos.com](https://epieos.com) and submit an email address through the web interface.

**What it is useful for:**

- Quickly checking if an email is linked to a public Google account (name, profile photo) as a fast identity-correlation lead.
- Cross-referencing an email against other services' registration traces.

**Limitations:**

- As a hosted third-party service, results depend entirely on Epieos' own data sources and uptime.
- A visible profile photo/name does not confirm the email owner's current identity or intent — verify independently.
- Free-tier usage may be rate-limited.

**Operational Safety:**

Because this is a hosted service, the email address you query is sent to a third party — avoid submitting sensitive case data unless you have reviewed Epieos' privacy policy and it fits your engagement's data-handling requirements. Do not use it to harvest data at scale in violation of its terms.
