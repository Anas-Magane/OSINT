# Yandex Images

**Category:** Image OSINT

**Purpose:** Reverse image search engine well known in the OSINT community for strong facial-similarity matching and broad coverage of Russian/CIS-region web content that other engines may not index as thoroughly.

**Website:** [yandex.com/images](https://yandex.com/images)

**Status:** ✅ Active | **License:** Free

**Installation:**

No installation required — web-based.

**Basic Usage:**

Go to [yandex.com/images](https://yandex.com/images), click the camera icon, and upload an image or paste an image URL.

**What it is useful for:**

- Reverse image search with different index coverage and matching behavior than Google/TinEye — useful as a second, independent source.
- Frequently cited for stronger face-matching results on portrait photos.
- Cross-referencing findings from other reverse-image tools.

**Limitations:**

- Interface and some features are optimized for Russian-language use.
- Like any reverse-image engine, results depend entirely on what has been indexed.
- A visual match is a lead, not proof of identity — always verify independently.

**Operational Safety:**

Uploaded images are sent to Yandex's servers — treat this the same as any third-party upload (avoid sensitive material, review the privacy policy). Use a research browser profile, not one signed into personal accounts.
